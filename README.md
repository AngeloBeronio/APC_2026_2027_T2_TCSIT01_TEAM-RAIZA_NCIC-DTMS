**NBI Clearance and Identification Center – Document Tracking and Management System**

A centralized web platform that replaces paper-based logbooks and disconnected division records at the NBI Clearance and Identification Center (NCIC) with a shared, searchable digital system.

## Project Overview
NCIC processes 500–1,000 document requests weekly across divisions. Currently, each division keeps separate paper logbooks with no shared visibility, making it slow to track requests, confirm hand-offs, or respond to status inquiries.

NCIC-DTMS addresses this by providing:
- A centralized, searchable database of document/request records
- A shared workflow tracker so all divisions see the same up-to-date status
- Secure, role-based access to scanned documents (no more relying on one staff member's computer)
- A management dashboard with automated reporting and AI-assisted summaries
- A limited, read-only public tracker for requesters to check progress without contacting staff

## Objectives
1. Retrieve document records within 10 seconds for 95% of test requests
2. Give all divisions a synchronized, consistent view of request status (98% target)
3. Let inquiries be resolved without contacting another division (98% target)
4. Give authorized staff reliable access to scanned files, regardless of whose computer scanned them (95% target)
5. Improve management's visibility into workload and delays (95% target; exact scope finalized in MSYADD1)
